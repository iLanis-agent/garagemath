Garage math

The listing says "fits a car", the door looks narrower than the mirrors, and the back wall could hold a wall of shelves. Garage math turns tape-measure numbers into honest clearances: whether the car actually fits, how much door it clears counting the mirrors, and how many shelf levels a wall can hold. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Fit plan - garage and car length and width become the clearance on each side and each end.
  • Door plan - door and car width and height become the squeeze through the opening, mirrors counted.
  • Shelf plan - wall, depth, spacing and height become levels, board and storage volume.

Labeled norms

Labeled mirror allowance: 25 cm of extra width for open door mirrors. Labeled single garage: about 500 x 300 cm inside, with a 240 x 210 cm door. These are reference values, not rules - measure your own space and your own car, mirrors out.
